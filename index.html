COLM 2026 AI Measurement Science Workshop

Measuring Reward Hacking and Reasoning-Answer Decoupling Under Confounded Optimization

Abhishek Mishra*, Armaan Sandhu*, Suyash Maniyar*

  • arXiv
  • Paper PDF
  • Poster
  • Course report (IPPO)
  • Code
  • Post-trained 10 LLMs (Qwen2.5, Llama 3.x) with GRPO on a confounded reward, inducing reward hacking.
  • Identified a backdoor-like failure that arises from fully correct training data and that endpoint metrics cannot detect, then designed an evaluation protocol with reasoning-answer decoupling and out-of-domain probes to catch it.
  • Red-teamed checkpoints: the hacked model chose the harmful option 84% of the time in the rewarded position, against 4% for controls and 0% once positions were swapped.

Under review, TMLR Feb – Aug 2026

Is the Refusal Direction Mostly Harmfulness?

  • Reproduced the claim that LLMs encode harmfulness and refusal separately on 8 models (Llama-3, Qwen2.5, Gemma-3, 3B to 27B) using nnsight activation interventions.
  • Showed the separation fails: after removing the refusal component, the harm direction keeps 76% of its causal effect.
  • Broke the proposed latent safety guard with a nanoGCG suffix, dropping recall to 40-74%.
